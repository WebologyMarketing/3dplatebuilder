What it does for your shop

Most plate shops still sell from a flat picture and a list of drop-downs. Customers guess, message you to check, or order the wrong thing. Your new shop shows them the plate they're buying.

  • More people finish their order

    Customers turn the plate, zoom in on the 4D edge and see the badge before they pay, so they buy instead of messaging you first.

  • Fewer wrong orders

    Every basket line spells it out: registration, sizes in mm, style, badge, border, extras and road legal or show plate. You make exactly what they saw.

  • Less admin

    Prices on one screen, documents collected at checkout, orders held until they're checked. Support for your shop is by email, with a record of everything.

Legal spacing, in millimetres

The builder places characters ink to ink, the way BS AU 145e measures them, and lays them out as the customer types. Current, prefix and suffix regs are spaced automatically; dateless and cherished regs stay as typed.

The builder helps with BS AU 145e layout. You stay responsible for the plates you make, your RNPS registration and checking your customers' documents.

Questions plate makers ask

Is the builder DVLA approved?

No. The software has no DVLA approval or certification, and we don't claim it does. It lays characters out to BS AU 145e measurements and puts your supplier name, postcode and the BS AU 145e mark in the footer, which saves you checking every layout by hand.

You stay responsible for the plates you make, your registration as a Registered Number Plate Supplier and checking your customers' documents.

I already have a website. Can you move it over?

Yes. It's included in Complete and an add-on on Starter and Pro: we copy your products, prices and pages across, and keep your domain. Tell us what you're on now when you request a demo.

I already run WooCommerce. Can I just add the builder?

Yes, that's Builder only. It's the same three plans without the website. We check your theme, checkout and hosting first, then install the builder, set your prices and test real orders with you. You keep your own hosting and look after WordPress updates; we keep the builder updated. It needs WordPress 6.5 or later.

Do I need to know WordPress?

No. We build the shop, host it and keep it updated. You change prices on one screen (WooCommerce → Plate Builder) and handle orders like any WooCommerce shop.

Who gets the money, and what are the fees?

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes to you and Stripe charges its fees to you directly.

How long is the contract?

Monthly plans have a minimum term of 12 months, then one month's notice. Your domain, products, customers and orders stay yours.
