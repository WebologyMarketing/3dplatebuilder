Sign-up to live

How it works

You run the workshop; we build the shop. From sign-up to your first order usually takes 2–4 weeks, all online and by email. No calls, no meetings. Start your shop.

Set-up steps

  1. Sign up and pay

    Choose your plan and pay the set-up fee and your first month by card. Your monthly payments then start when your shop goes live. It takes about five minutes.

    Day 1

  2. Add your details and prices

    Your account shows what to do next: company and contact details, logo and colours, and your RNPS registration and ID for us to check. Tick the styles, sizes, badges, borders and extras you sell, set your prices, add your own extras (and products on Pro and Complete), then publish.

    In your own time

  3. We build your website

    Your supplier name and postcode go in the plate footer, exactly as registered. Your site is designed to your plan: a one-page shop in your colours on Starter, a custom home page and a page for each character style on Pro, a fully custom site on Complete. You check a private preview and ask for changes by email.

    Usually 1–2 weeks

  4. Connect Stripe and test

    You open (or already have) your own Stripe account. We connect it, switch on Apple Pay and Google Pay, and run real test orders on your preview.

    Usually 2–3 working days

  5. Go live

    We point your domain at the new shop, switch Stripe to live and watch the first orders come in with you.

    Usually 1 working day

What we need from you

Get these together and set-up goes quickly. Your account shows a checklist as soon as you sign up.

  • Your pricesYou set them yourself in your account, with sensible starting prices already filled in.
  • Your RNPS registration and IDWe verify them before we build. Your supplier name and postcode go in the plate footer exactly as registered.
  • Your logoSVG or a large PNG, if you have one.
  • A Stripe accountIn your business's name. We'll help you set it up if you don't have one.
  • Your domainWho it's registered with, so we can point it at the new shop.
  • Document contactsThe email, WhatsApp number and postal address customers should send documents to.
  • Collection and fittingYour address, opening hours and fitting price, if you offer them.

After you're live

We keep looking after it, so you can get on with making plates.

  • Prices and products are yours to changeIn your account, any time. Publish and your shop updates within minutes.
  • UpdatesWe update WordPress, WooCommerce and the builder for you, and test them first.
  • SupportBy email, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Support tickets from your shop's admin are coming soon.
  • Room to growMove up a plan, or add a custom style when you start making a new finish.
