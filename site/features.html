Features

Everything the builder does today, in plain English. Each feature is tagged with the plan it comes in. Anything we haven't built yet is listed at the bottom as coming soon.

Starter

A real 3D plate

Not a picture with text on top. Each plate is modelled in 3D, at real size, with studio lighting that shows off gel domes and 4D edges.

  • Turn, zoom and panDrag with a finger or mouse, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to zoom in. Customers can turn the plate right round.
  • Front and rearWhite front and yellow rear plates, one tap apart.
  • Close-ups on cuePick a character style and the camera swings in at an angle to show its profile.
  • Light on phonesIt only draws while something moves, so an idle builder doesn't drain the battery.
Try it in the live demo
Starter

Legal layout as they type

The builder applies UK registration spacing and the Charles Wright characters while the customer types, and tells them if something won't work.

  • Automatic spacingCurrent (AB12 CDE), prefix (A123 BCD) and suffix (ABC 123D) regs are spaced for them. Dateless and cherished regs stay as typed.
  • Fit checks"This registration doesn't fit a motorbike plate with a badge" appears before they order, not after you've cut it.
  • Road legal or show plateA status badge shows which they're getting.
  • Your footer on every plateSupplier name, postcode and the BS AU 145e mark, as the standard requires.

The builder helps with BS AU 145e layout. You stay responsible for compliance and for checking customers' documents.

Starter

Seven character styles

Each style is modelled in 3D, layer by layer, at its true depth. Every one keeps the character footprint at its legal size, and raised styles cast real shadows on the plate.

Tap a style to open it in the live demo.

  • PrintedFlat ink under the acrylic.
  • 3D GelA 2.2 mm domed resin over each character.
  • 4D 3mmLaser-cut acrylic with a polished edge.
  • 4D 6mmTwo 3 mm layers, 6 mm deep.
  • 4D Gel 3mm3 mm acrylic with a gel dome on top.
  • 4D Gel 6mm6 mm acrylic with a gel dome on top.
  • RetroA 5 mm pressed chamfer, like classic plates.

An app-like builder on phones

On phones the builder fills the screen like an app. The plate stays pinned at the top and the options come as cards underneath.

  • Step cardsReg and style, plate size, badge, border, add-ons. Swipe or tap Next.
  • Progress bar and sticky buy barThe total and the next button stay at the bottom, and the keyboard never covers them.
  • Back worksThe app bar's arrow and the phone's own back button both close the builder.

On Starter, the builder sits in the page on phones instead.

Complete

Checkout inside the builder

After add-ons, the builder turns into your real WooCommerce checkout, so every payment method, delivery rule and check works exactly as it does now.

  • Apple Pay and Google PayThrough Stripe, shown under Proceed to checkout and Place order.
  • Delivery or collect in storeYour WooCommerce shipping options, including collection.
  • KlarnaHands off to Klarna if you offer it through your payment set-up.
  • Order confirmationCustomers see their confirmation without leaving the builder.
Complete

Proof of ID and entitlement

As a registered supplier you must see one proof of ID and one proof of entitlement before handing over road-legal plates. The builder builds that into checkout. Show plates skip it.

  • Upload now or send laterA photo or PDF of each document at checkout, or send them by email, WhatsApp, post or at collection.
  • Real file checksJPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or PDF up to 10 MB, checked by their contents, not just the file name.
  • Awaiting documents statusPaid orders that need documents wait here instead of Processing, and still count as paid in your reports.
  • Staff check panelStaff open the documents through private links, record what they saw and move the order on. Who checked it, and when, is saved on the order.
  • Private and tidyFiles are never in the media library, and they're deleted automatically after a set time.
Complete

In-store fitting

Fitting is an add-on in the builder. Choosing it sets the order to collection, and the builder shows the fitting charge as "added at checkout".

The fitting charge and delivery choice cards at checkout are set up for each shop; a packaged version is coming soon.
