Set-up, hosting and terms

The short version. The full terms are in our terms of service.

  • Set-up feePaid once, before we start. For website packages it covers designing and building your site, copying your products and prices, adding your supplier details and logo, connecting Stripe and testing orders with you. For builder only, it covers checking your site, installing and setting up the builder, and testing orders.
  • Monthly plansBilled monthly in advance. Minimum term 12 months, then one month's notice.
  • HostingWebsite packages run on our managed servers, with an SSL certificate and backups every day. Builder only runs on your own hosting.
  • Updates and supportWe keep the builder up to date on every plan, and WordPress and WooCommerce too on website packages. Support by email, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. No phone lines: everything is in writing. Support tickets from your shop's admin are coming soon.
  • Monthly changesComplete includes 2 hours of changes a month. Unused hours don't roll over. Extra work is charged at the design rate below.
  • Changing planMove up a plan at any time; we'll switch on the extra features and do the extra website work. Builder-only shops can move to a website package too. Moving down a plan takes effect at the end of your minimum term.
  • Card feesPayments go to your own Stripe account, and Stripe charges its fees to you directly.
  • It's your shopYour domain, products, customers and orders are yours. If you leave, we'll give you a full copy of your site, products and orders, and help you move your domain.
